Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 16, 2026

Your privacy matters to us. This Privacy Policy explains what information Spendly collects, how we use it, and the choices you have. By using Spendly, you agree to the practices described here.

1. Data We Collect

We collect only the information needed to run the Service:

  • Account information — your name, email address, and a securely hashed password.
  • Expense data — the amounts, categories, dates, and descriptions you enter.
  • Basic usage data — technical details such as browser type and access times, used to keep the Service secure and working properly.

We do not collect bank account details, card numbers, or connect to your financial institutions.

2. How We Use Your Data

We use your information to:

  • Create and manage your account.
  • Store, display, and summarize your expenses.
  • Maintain the security and reliability of the Service.
  • Respond to your questions and support requests.

We do not sell your personal information, and we do not use your expense data for advertising.

3. Data Storage

Your data is stored on secured servers, and passwords are never stored in plain text. We keep your information for as long as your account is active. If you delete your account, your personal and expense data will be removed from our systems.

While we take reasonable measures to protect your data, no method of storage or transmission is completely secure.

4. Third Party Services

Spendly may rely on a small number of trusted third-party providers, such as hosting services and web font providers, to operate the Service. These providers only receive the information necessary to perform their function and are expected to protect it.

We do not share your expense data with third parties except where required by law.

5. Your Rights

You can view, update, or delete your data at any time from within the app, or ask us to do so on your behalf.

6. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. When we do, we will revise the "Last updated" date above.

7. Contact Us

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy or how your data is handled, please reach out to the Spendly team.
